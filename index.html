What this widget does

For Monthly_Sales, it uses the products in Products and the distinct month dates already in Monthly Sales; it creates missing pairs and lets Grist's CE and Units formulas calculate. To extend the calendar beyond existing months, add those months first.

For Account_YoY_Data, it uses Accounts and Years. If Account YoY already has duplicate keys, it skips that part until those records are reviewed. It never deletes or rewrites records.
