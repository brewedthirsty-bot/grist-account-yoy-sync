What this widget does

It reads Accounts.CanonicalID, every value in Years.Year, and the existing CanonicalID + Year pairs in Account_YoY_Data. It inserts only combinations that do not already exist.

If the target table has an Account text column, the widget also fills it from Accounts.Account. Formula columns such as Account Ref, YTD CEs, Prior Year YTD CEs, Opportunity, and Priority are left for Grist to calculate.
